Audit notes

  • Accuracy at the default 0.5 cut-off looks excellent, but a missed cancer and a false alarm are not equal mistakes.
  • Lowering the threshold catches more cancers at the cost of more false alarms. The right setting is a clinical decision, not a statistical one.
  • The dataset has no demographic fields, so nobody can say whether it represents patients in Lagos, Abuja or Atlanta. Always test a model on the population it will serve.
Data: UCI Breast Cancer Wisconsin (Diagnostic), public. Model: standardized logistic regression, 5-fold cross-validated predictions. Educational analysis, not medical advice.
